Privacy Policy for Potato Popper

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Potato Popper (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information.

What data we collect

The App only does the following:

  • High score – The App stores your high score locally on your device so it can be shown the next time you play.
  • Device rotation / orientation – The App reads your device’s rotation sensors (accelerometer / gyroscope) so the game can respond to how you tilt or turn your phone.

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other data that can identify you.

How we use the data

  • High score is used only to display your best score inside the App.
  • Device rotation is used only for gameplay (controlling the game with the movement of your device).

Data sharing

We do not share any data with third parties.
We do not use advertising networks, analytics services, or any other external services that collect data.

Data storage

All data (your high score) stays only on your own device. We never send it to any server.

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the App handles data, we will update this Privacy Policy.
